# Angle: Top 5 Mistakes to Avoid in GTA 6

**Format:** mid-length countdown (target 9-10 min, ~1,850 words). The countdown *is* the chapter structure: each mistake is one system, and each one causes the next.

## Spine
In GTA 5, a mistake cost you a hospital trip. In GTA 6, a mistake follows you.

Ending version: "GTA 5 asked how fast you could get back into the action. GTA 6 asks what you're still carrying when you do."

## Systems → chapters (ordered so each causes the next)
| # | Mistake | System | Bridge out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Walking around with your cash | Money (wallet vs bank) | The fastest way to lose that cash is getting caught. → Heat |
| 4 | Thinking a new hat fools the cops | Heat / police memory | The cops can forget your shirt. Your reputation doesn't forget anything. → Criminal Profile |
| 3 | Living in the Psycho tier | Criminal Profile | Irredeemable is permanent, unless you have a save from before it. Do you? → Saves |
| 2 | Trusting the autosave | Save system (no replay) | And there's one mission you really want a save in front of. → (fast round, then) Tourist Trap |
| — | Fast round: four quick ones | Dye packs, registering cars, masks vs CCTV, buying a car late in Ch.1 | "That last one is a hint." → #1 |
| 1 | Starting Tourist Trap unprepared | Chapter 1 point of no return | → Ending |

## Cold-open contrasts (draft)
- GTA 5: die, wake up outside a hospital, carry on. GTA 6: die, and the cash in your pocket is gone.
- GTA 5: lose the cops, they forget you. GTA 6: they remember your shirt.
- GTA 5: replay any mission from the pause menu. GTA 6: no replays. Not even after the credits.
- GTA 6: one mission in Chapter 1 that takes your car, your house and your wallet. (withheld → #1)
